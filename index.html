About Magrana

Magrana is an anagram puzzle game where you form words from a set of letters to progress through increasingly challenging levels.

Dictionary

The word list is based on SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), specifically levels 10-70, which includes common to moderately common English words.

Note: Not all possible English words are included in the dictionary. The word list is curated to exclude very obscure or rare terms.
